# The Sapote–Mamey Encyclopedia (retired)

Find the retired encyclopedia and current alternatives.  
This page is for readers following a historical reference.

The thirteen Encyclopedia volumes and their currency record are retired. They were a wiki copy of an early Technical Manual, and their settings had fallen out of date. They are kept as a historical record, each with a retirement banner, in [docs/history/wiki_encyclopedia/](../docs/history/wiki_encyclopedia/). Start with the [currency record](../docs/history/wiki_encyclopedia/Encyclopedia-Currency.md), which lists the few checks that were refreshed.
