# The Sapote–Mamey Encyclopedia (retired)

Find the retired encyclopedia and current alternatives.  
This page is for readers following a historical reference.

The thirteen Encyclopedia volumes and their currency record are retired. They were a wiki copy of an early Technical Manual, and their settings had fallen out of date. They are kept unchanged, as a historical record, in [docs/history/wiki_encyclopedia/](../docs/history/wiki_encyclopedia/Encyclopedia-Volume-I.md).

For current information, read the [User Manual](User-Manual.md), the [Glossary](Glossary.md), the [Quick Guide](Quick-Guide.md) and the [Concepts Q&A](Concepts-Q-and-A.md).
