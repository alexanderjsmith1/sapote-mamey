__TITLE__

Each row is one specific enzyme: a set of cohort proteins that share at least about 50% identity over at least 80% of their length. Open a row to see every gene in it.

Context. Each group and family shows what that kind of enzyme does in general (reference text, not a finding about these genes) and the evidence from our packages: the antiSMASH region classes the genes sit in, and the MIBiG clusters that hold the genes' best per-gene hits, counted gene by gene. A MIBiG match means a similar gene, not that the strain makes that compound. A blank means no saved hit in the available input, not a negative search.

How to read it. Strains and genes count the members. BLASTp is the median, over the members, of each gene's mean identity to its top 10 hits in ClusteredNR or nr; lower means further from anything deposited, and values under 70% are marked. To centroid is each member's identity to the group's reference protein. A group is sequence similarity, not a shared product.
