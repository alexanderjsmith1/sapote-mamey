Per-genome identity, gene and domain summaries

Points. Filled isolate points: the closest public-genome, SID or MIBiG protein is below 70% amino-acid identity. Open points: 70% or more. Points may be proteins or domains. Public and SID points are cluster representatives, not genome counts. Identity is measured against the pooled reference, SID and MIBiG search background; hiding a context group does not recalculate it.

Run 7 PCoA. The final genome set: 1,645 public genomes, 116 SID genomes and 45 isolates, actinomycetes only. Classical principal coordinates of DIAMOND identity distances; the three displayed axes are a projection and may distort the original distances. Use the original distances for statistical comparisons; visual separation alone does not establish a cohort difference.

Run 4 TriMap. The earlier run's points and distances, laid out in three dimensions by TriMap. TriMap keeps each protein's near neighbours better than PCoA does, but distances between separate groups carry no meaning. AS-102 and the cleaned AS-815 were not in run 4, so the run 4 view has 42 isolate genomes.

Left out of both views. AS-813 (contaminated assembly), run 4's raw AS-815 assembly, and AS-956's second-part regions. AS-320, from a lichen, is drawn with the mosses.

Resistance sets. Run 7's 19 CARD-family sets wait for their family names from the M5; run 4's carry theirs.

Reading it. Distance from known proteins describes sequence only. It says nothing about the compound a cluster makes or about activity.
