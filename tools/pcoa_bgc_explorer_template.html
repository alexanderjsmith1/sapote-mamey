Points sit where amino-acid identity puts them (all-against-all protein search, classical PCoA). Nearness to a reference or MIBiG protein is similarity of sequence, not a shared product. Gene labels come from antiSMASH and are hints at function. MIBiG hits, where shown, are DIAMOND blastp of each gene against MIBiG proteins; a close MIBiG gene is similarity, not the same compound. NCBI and Swiss-Prot hits, where shown, come from a copy of the BLASTp store and are bound to a gene by strain, locus tag and protein length, or by exact sequence; an NCBI hit is nearest-neighbour similarity, not characterization. A BGC number is shown only when a Mamey package was built from the same antiSMASH result as the region file; otherwise the region is on identity hold.
