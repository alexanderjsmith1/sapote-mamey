Population, exclusions and counting rules

. Only the explicitly pinned census is included; alternate populations are not merged. Every exact region remains in the locus denominator, including regions without admitted domain annotations. Class labels are source-bound multi-label locus annotations; a protein in multiple classes is counted in each selected stratum. Class strata must not be summed.

Patterns contain at least two distinct labels for unordered sets or two domain occurrences for ordered arrangements. Unordered sets discard multiplicity. Ordered arrangements preserve repeats and use nucleotide coordinates along the parent protein's strand. Overlapping domain intervals or unknown strands hold ordered results. This is not gene order, pathway order or module chemistry.

Only source-protein-and-geometry-bound aSDomain or PFAM_domain records with a unique exact parent are admitted; the two surfaces are never pooled. Exact coordinate-and-label duplicates collapse within a protein. Physical proteins are identified by strain, full contig, parent coordinates, strand, tag and sequence hash, and counted once across overlapping regions. Each region retains its own membership, so locus prevalence includes correlated overlapping regions and is not an independent-sample estimate. Boundary-context-only genes are excluded.

Minimum support is the number of distinct matching strains, after filters; default 2. Setting it to 1 exposes singletons without promoting them to novelty. Zero annotation means unknown within this scope, not biological absence. No housekeeping/mobile-domain filtering or functional interpretation is invented; source labels are preserved.
